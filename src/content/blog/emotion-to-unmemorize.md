Frustration is the emotion I'd like to unmemorize. Frustration that leads to inactivity, stiffness, hopelessness, anger, regret.

My head gets tense, my body contracts, I become grateful for the fat I've accumulated and its ability to desensitize me. I feel like corned beef, manufactured to be unthinking and uncaring. I want to be weak, helpless, unable to find my own way. I definitely don't want to solve the problem but instead wallow in it, finding purpose in the obstruction, feeling important because I work with challenging and frustrating facts. I become unconsciously defensive. The feeling becomes persistent and I stop thinking about positive things, things that I can be excited about. Rather than planning a positive future I instead stay stuck — a willing pawn for anyone who wants to move me around.

Mentally I give up thinking. I become dumb and unable to see options. I treat the frustration as an end point and try to then control someone else, sometimes anyone else, as a way of disconnecting from the feeling, so I can then feel something more like anger than shame.

The two key thinking features are helplessness and blame.

## Admitting and declaring

I've been a coward. I am caring less and less. I have let go of much of what I thought was important. I am stiff and blocked, and have grown something in my solar plexus that seems to help me accept this state, and to justify it. It protects me from feeling too much or caring too much. In not caring I find no reason to do more, but instead polish where I am currently at, as if that will be sufficient — which I don't think it will be.

## Surrender

Universal mind within me, I forgive my frustrations, my cowardice, my dullness, and my inability to be more, and I give these to you. I trust that you have the mind to resolve them much better than I could. Arrange the players in my world, ones I know and ones I don't know, so that doors open for me. Innate intelligence, I release my barriers and my self-pity to you. I have mismanaged my inner thoughts and actions for long enough. I allow you to intervene and provide a greater life, in a way that is just right for me.

Thank you.

---

## The process

1. **Induction** — continue to become more and more used to this process as a way of accessing the subconscious.
2. **Recognize** what I want to change about myself in mind and body, "recognizing" my limitations. Define the emotion that I want to unmemorize and look at the associated attitude that is driven by that emotion.
3. **Admit** to a higher power within me who I have been, what I want to change about myself, and what I have been hiding.
4. **Declare** what emotion I am releasing so as to free the body from the mind and break the bonds to the elements in my environment.
5. **Surrender** this self-limiting state to a greater mind and ask that it be resolved in a way that is right for me.
