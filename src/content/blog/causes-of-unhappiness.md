## Action

I acted inconsistent with my values.

Tricky since we don't always know our values, which results in delayed unhappiness. Or we don't recognize the discrepancy as we act. This can be exacerbated by addictions or situations that result in us being unaware of truth.

It can also mean we didn't act.

Actions like consumption often have a direct biochemical result.

## Thought

I thought something that was inconsistent with my values.

Same as the last — we sometimes aren't aware or don't recognize even if aware.

Thoughts are particularly challenging because they are fleeting and we are generally unaware. This becomes particularly apparent once we do become aware and then recognize how unaware we were previously. Like all things there is also always another level. Ideal thought is unattainable, which is, of course, why happiness is also challenging. It is also why happiness demands a continuing practice.

## Event

We usually think it is events that make us unhappy. We believe this until we learn otherwise — which, unfortunately, is often being numb to the events of our lives.

Sometimes we just recognize that we are unhappy and aren't clear on the cause. Tracking them back, I find they are always one of the above.
