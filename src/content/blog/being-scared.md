We are going to die, as is everyone we know and love. Being scared seems like it should be the natural reaction. So, what you hope to have learned by 50 is:

1. Courage to do what you aspire to do.
2. Counterbalance the fear with the capacity for good feelings.

Most of us don't have these skills. Instead we read the news to drown our courage, consume entertainment to forget our aspirations, and wear ourselves down with food, work and drink rather than engage in the activities that will generate positive feelings.

The answer, unfortunately, isn't one thing but many things:

1. Clarify your purpose and use it as a way to transcend fear.
2. Care for your body, removing the capacity for addiction.
3. Build and work towards a positive vision.
4. Build relationships that you will cherish.

This morning though I feel fear. In my belly. Because I haven't done the work I should have done, I'm not sufficiently organized, I have too many unread emails, and I created some chafe yesterday that I will need to clean up.

On the last one I need to default to kind, especially when it is hard.

Interesting that I feel more fear when I am rested. Probably means I don't have the capacity for fear when my body isn't rested and when I am distracting myself with news and tv.

Do I cut them out? Do I still need to ask myself this after 30 years. Have I gotten a lot out of them over those 30 years? Nighttime me makes poor choices, even if I want to make believe that he makes good intuitive choices.
