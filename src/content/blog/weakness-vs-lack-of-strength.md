description: 'A short note on making weakness more complicated than it needs to be.'
pubDate: 2026-05-03

---

We know how to get stronger. We do it through the deliberate exercising of strength in the area in which we want to get stronger.

We often try to make weakness complicated. We blame our past, others, our circumstances.
