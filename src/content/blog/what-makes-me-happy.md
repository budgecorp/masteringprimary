I love the pinkish warm glow I get after I've done something hard and can roll around in it a bit. I had it this week after a hard run and a sit in the ocean (can also be from work). Possibilities opened up and I was eager to do more, to be useful.

I'm energized by nimble conversations that roll with humour and require a bit of a stretch (a risk, an exposure) from both of us to stay in the groove.

I feel lucky when I relax into being a partner, making good money, driving a nice car, feeling fit, while wearing my canvas Converse, a cotton hoodie and banged-up jeans, and my body still moving like I might have game.

I'm happy when I'm pushing myself physically (consistent hard running, cold tub every day) and find a spark not dissimilar to what I get from drinking.

I feel usefulness and pride when my kids say something funny, or smart, and they experience joy in their expression.

I find deep satisfaction when no one is around at night and I dance to something funky long enough to sweat.

I feel smooth when I've been meditating twice a day and gotten enough sleep.

I feel clean when I fast for a few days and still exercise.

I'm most optimistic when I'm in the habit of writing — especially if I am creating, even if it is poetry.

I feel most powerful when I'm engaged in the world, paying attention to its signals, and letting them guide me.
