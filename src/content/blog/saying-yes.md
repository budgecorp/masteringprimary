The easiest choice is usually to say no; to unwanted interruptions, more work, change, someone else's success, opportunities to be more genuine, requests, risks, and to our practice. Mastering primary is predicated on saying yes to a daily practice — it isn't simply important, it is necessary.

The process is complicated by the nature of decisions. Saying yes very often means saying no to something else. It is therefore necessary to define the values and intentions through which we can make decisions. From a practice perspective — and let's go back to this as a microcosm in which to understand the bigger challenge — saying yes means selecting to encourage our inclination to practice at 5 in the morning, which means saying no to having a drink at night. It means saying yes to healthy goal-supportive food (animal-free in the case of a yogi) so we reduce stiffness and the need to expend lots of energy digesting food.

It means saying yes to waking up at 5am and sweating our way through a hard practice, intentionally working our bodies, focusing our attention, gradually connecting our spirit to something greater than ourselves. When you see a long-time practitioner drenched and working through their practice they are clearly not working out just their bodies — they are trying to refine themselves and connect to something more genuine. It is clearly very hard work whose purpose is understood and whose yoke has been gratefully accepted.

How does this translate to our daily jobs? It means saying yes within the context of our principles and intentions, the lines that define our commitments, while trying to recognize the limits these impose, taking responsibility for the impact, and adjusting as required to the inevitably changing situation.

It also means saying yes to our limits, to where we are starting from, regardless of how big of a gong show we are, so that we don't hurt ourselves and can therefore practice again, and so we don't delude ourselves and can therefore recognize the hard work we have committed to in the future.
