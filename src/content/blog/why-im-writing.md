I am writing this because I aspire to more.

I expect most people hitting fifty are wondering whether the moment where life is full of promise has passed. I believe it hasn't, and I am going to prove it.

And, my kids are watching and absorbing what I do and how I think. I see in them the same desire to dream and accomplish, the same impulse to avoid the inevitable pain, and I want to provide them with a better model.

I'm going to do this by reflecting back on my life so far and picking up again those things that excited me most, scared me most, and which I therefore didn't do.

I can point to a few of these points of inflection, where I picked the safe route. Potentially a path of integrity and value, but they were also the ones that didn't risk breaking me, or my heart.

I can also point to points when I picked the path of courage.

At fifty, I can say that safety rather than courage is my default. Making improvements rather than finding new paths. This often leaves me sad, missing enthusiasm critical to the next fifty years of my life.

I see the way forward as requiring that I change. That I pick things that will change me and then persist until they change me. The list of things that have moved me and offered an opportunity to change is short:

1. Being an Ashtanga practitioner, doing 3rd and 4th series.
2. Writing novels and making a living.
3. Permissive sex which I'd observed in others to be a consuming illusion.

At some point in our lives, we have probably all seen the need to go through, and stopped short. I think this is going to be the rest of the journey through.

And so, I am going to do these things and record the experience, providing a guide to others who are aspiring and too scared.
