description: 'Starting the day with caffeine sets the stage for an unproductive day spent trying to push back out of disharmony.'
pubDate: 2026-05-03
draft: true
---

Starting the day with caffeine sets the stage for an unproductive day where I am humming just above ideal — a condition that I then try to push myself out of for the rest of the day.

These pushes tend to be destructive and involve food, indulging in frustration, and being miles away from a happy place where I'd feel blessed, on path, or in harmony. Almost as if I have a need for disharmony that could jolt me back.

I end the day tired but agitated, caffeinated, and in strong need of distraction.
